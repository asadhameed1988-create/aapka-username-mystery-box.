# Privacy Policy – Mystery Box: Unlock the Unknown

Developer: Free Games Station
Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL
Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what the game "Mystery Box: Unlock the Unknown" (the "game") does with information.
It describes only what the game's code actually does.

## Playing offline
The game works without an account and without internet. Your progress, settings, prizes and
purchase flags are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

## Online features (optional)
Daily and weekly challenges, leaderboards and progress backup use our game server when online
play is on. Online play can be switched off at any time in Profile and Privacy. When it is on,
the server stores:
- a random player number created for your device (not your name, phone number or contacts);
- the display name you choose, if any;
- your results on daily and weekly boxes (time and hints used) for the leaderboards;
- a copy of your game progress, so it can be restored on another device;
- your email address and a protected (hashed) password, only if you choose to register.

## Anonymous usage statistics (optional)
When online play is on, the game counts events such as "a box was completed" and "an ad was
shown", and a short performance sample (frames per second, operating system name, device model).
These numbers are used to fix problems and balance difficulty. They can be switched off in
Profile and Privacy.

## Advertising
The game shows ads through Google AdMob, never while you are answering a question.
Google and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and
other data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's policies:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites .
Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

## Purchases
Purchases (such as removing ads or buying hints) are handled by Google Play.
We never receive your payment card details.

## Sharing
We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers
needed to run the game: Google AdMob, Google Play and our server hosting provider.

## Keeping and deleting your data
You can delete your online account and everything stored about it at any time in the game:
Profile and Privacy > Delete my online account. You can also ask us by email at CONTACT_EMAIL.
Leaderboard results and the progress backup are deleted together with the account.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal
information from them.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be published at this address.
