## Playing offline
The game works without an account. Your progress, settings and purchases flags are stored only on your device
and are removed when you uninstall the game.

## Online play (optional, can be switched off in Profile and Privacy)
If online play is on, our server stores:
- a random player number created for your device (not your name, phone number or contacts);
- the display name you choose, if any;
- your results on daily and weekly boxes (time and hints used) for the leaderboards;
- a copy of your game progress, so it can be restored on another device;
- your email address and a protected (hashed) password, only if you choose to register.

## Anonymous usage statistics (optional, can be switched off)
We count events such as "a box was completed", "an ad was shown" and a short performance sample
(frames per second, operating system name, device model). These are used to fix problems and balance difficulty.

## Advertising
The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use device identifiers
(such as the advertising ID) and other data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's own policies:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent
before personalised ads are shown. Ads never appear while you are solving a puzzle.

## Purchases
Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.

## Sharing
We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to run the game
(hosting provider: [HOSTING PROVIDER], Google AdMob, Google Play).

## Keeping and deleting your data
You can delete your online account and everything stored about it at any time in the game:
Profile and Privacy > Delete my online account. You can also ask us at [CONTACT EMAIL] or at [DELETION WEB PAGE URL].
Leaderboard results and progress are deleted with the account.
