## Full description – English
Step into an old Arabian house of wisdom, where every carved box is locked with three questions.
Answer them all and the box opens.

THREE SECTIONS, ENDLESS CHAPTERS
• Islamic Knowledge – the Holy Quran (surahs, ayahs, paras), Seerah, Islamic months and basic
  Fiqh Hanafi questions.
• General Knowledge – Pakistan, capitals, continents, neighbours and the countries of the world.
• Maths – from quick sums to equations, harder every two chapters.

NO REPEATED QUESTIONS
More than three million different questions. A question comes back only after every other
question of its kind has been asked.

PRIZES TO COLLECT
Win a brass seal or a picture for every five boxes you open: bronze, silver and gold seals and
old Arabian artwork for your collection.

MORE
• Daily challenge and achievements
• Hints remove a wrong answer
• English and Urdu
• Plays offline

No ads while you are answering a question.
